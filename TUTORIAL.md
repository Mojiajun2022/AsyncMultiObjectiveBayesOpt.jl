# Tutorial

The complete executable tutorial is available in two forms:

- [GitHub source](docs/src/tutorial.md)
- [Rendered documentation](https://Mojiajun2022.github.io/AsyncMultiObjectiveBayesOpt.jl/tutorial/)

It includes:

- Schaffer N.1 with an analytical Pareto curve;
- a convex two-dimensional objective and decision-space front;
- standard and high-accuracy ZDT1 configurations;
- mixed minimization and maximization;
- a three-objective MPI program;
- an expensive simulation template;
- result inspection, CSV export, tests, and plot reproduction.

All source programs are also available under [`examples/`](examples) and
[`benchmark/`](benchmark).
